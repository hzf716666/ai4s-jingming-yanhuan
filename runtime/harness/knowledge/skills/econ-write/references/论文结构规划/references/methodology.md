# Paper Structure Planning — Methodology
> Extracted from ARC Stage 16: `_paper_writing.py` `_execute_paper_outline()`

## IMRAD Structure with Section Word Budgets
- Abstract: 180-220 words
- Introduction: 800-1000 words (broad→narrow→gap→contribution funnel)
- Related Work: 600-800 words (thematic organization, not list)
- Method: 1000-1500 words (sufficient for replication)
- Experiments: 800-1200 words
- Results: 600-800 words (present without interpretation)
- Discussion: 400-600 words
- Limitations: 200-300 words
- Conclusion: 200-300 words
- Broader Impact: 200-400 words

## Template Selection
- Conference templates: NeurIPS 2025, ICML 2026, ICLR 2026
- Markdown → LaTeX conversion with math, tables, cross-refs

## Quality Constraints
- Figures/tables referenced in text BEFORE they appear
- Reference list matches in-text citations exactly
- Abstract accurately reflects final content
- Methods enable independent replication
